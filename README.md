# CheckStitch

Write the checklist once. Get every reminder in one tap.

CheckStitch turns a checklist into Apple Reminders — one reminder per item, in one tap.

**iPhone · iPad · Mac · Apple Watch**

---

CheckStitch is the missing bridge between the list you wrote and the reminders you actually need. Name a checklist, add your items, and run it: each item becomes its own Apple Reminder, in the list you choose. No more typing the same thing ten times, and no more half-finished lists lost in Notes. Stitch the two together and get on with your day.

## Why you'll love it

- **One tap, a whole list.** Running a checklist creates a reminder for every item at once, so the typing is finished before you start.
- **Your order survives.** Number Reminders prefixes each title with its position — "1. Buy milk", "2. Call the plumber" — so the sequence you wrote is the sequence you see in Reminders.
- **Notes come along.** Anything you type under an item rides into the reminder's note, so the details stay attached to the task.

## Everything you need, nothing you don't

CheckStitch does one job properly. Write a checklist, decide where it goes, and run it — as often as you like:

- One checklist, many items. Each item becomes exactly one reminder, with the item's name as the title.
- Pick the destination. Send a checklist's reminders to your Reminders inbox or to any list you already have.
- Number Reminders, optionally. Prefix each title with its position when the order matters.
- Notes carry over. Anything you type under an item becomes the reminder's note.
- Duplicate and reuse. Turn a routine into a checklist and run it again next week, with no retyping.
- Export and import. Back up your checklists or move them between devices from Settings.
- Make it yours. Choose a System, Light, or Dark appearance and, if you like, a random nature wallpaper that refreshes regularly or on demand.

## Thoughtful by design

- Reminders are created through Apple Reminders and stay on your device or in your own iCloud account. They are never sent to the author or to any third party.
- CheckStitch only ever creates reminders. It never reads, edits, completes, or deletes them afterwards.
- Your checklists live on your device and sync through your own iCloud — no account to create, nothing to sign in to.
- No analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. The only network traffic is the optional background wallpaper.
